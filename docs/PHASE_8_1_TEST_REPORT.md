## 4. Evidence verification

For each response, the title and feed description were concatenated and every stored claim's
`evidence` value was checked as a literal substring:

```text
article=16  claims=5  nonliteral=0  sourceEvidence literal=true
article=131 claims=2  nonliteral=0  sourceEvidence literal=true
article=44  claims=4  nonliteral=0  sourceEvidence literal=true
```

The claim filter worked. Narrative fields are a separate matter: article 131 was heavily
under-explained, and articles 16 and 44 contain small unsupported inferences outside the
claims array. Those defects are detailed in `REAL_AI_QUALITY_REPORT.md`; they do not invalidate
the evidence result.

## 9. Conclusion

Phase 8.1 is complete for the approved scope. The real integration, claim-evidence gate,
cached reuse and per-user recommendation isolation all work. The remaining defect is quality,
not safety: the research abstract was mostly replaced with placeholders, and some narrative
fields contain unsupported elaboration. Those should be addressed before treating AI
explanations as production-quality for every feed type.
